又称自毁容貌（self-mutilation）综合征
## 致病机理
- HGPRT是体内核酸补救合成途径的关键酶
- HGPRT缺陷使次黄嘌呤、鸟嘌呤向相应核苷酸的转化受阻，底物在体内堆积（包括神经系统） 
- 尿酸增多（结石、肾脏损伤）
## 临床表现
The features of Lesch-Nyhan syndrome (LNS) are **intellectual disability, spastic cerebral palsy, choreoathetosis, uric acid urinary stones, and self-destructive biting of fingers and lips.** Megaloblastic anemia has been found in some patients (summary by [van der Zee et al., 1968](https://www.omim.org/entry/300322?search=Lesch-Nyhan%20syndrome&highlight=%22lesch%20nyhan%22%2C%28syndrome%7Csyndromic%29%2Cleschnyhan#41), [Madeo et al., 2019](https://www.omim.org/entry/300322?search=Lesch-Nyhan%20syndrome&highlight=%22lesch%20nyhan%22%2C%28syndrome%7Csyndromic%29%2Cleschnyhan#24)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=4173010+31182398)

- 一般为男性 
- 3-4个月神经系统症状：激惹不安，烦躁，运动发育迟缓 
- 1岁后出现舞蹈样手足徐动，肌张力高，下肢呈剪刀样交叉 
- 约2-3岁起，表现强迫性自我摧残行为，多数智商低于65；患儿精神发育迟滞，强直性大脑性瘫痪
