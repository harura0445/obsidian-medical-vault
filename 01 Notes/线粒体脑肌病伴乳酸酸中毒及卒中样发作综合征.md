# 线粒体脑肌病伴乳酸酸中毒及卒中样发作综合征
MELAS syndrome, comprising **mitochondrial myopathy, encephalopathy, lactic acidosis, and stroke-like episodes**, is a genetically heterogeneous mitochondrial disorder with a variable clinical phenotype. The disorder is accompanied by features of central nervous system involvement, including seizures, hemiparesis, hemianopsia, cortical blindness, and episodic vomiting ([Pavlakis et al., 1984](https://www.omim.org/entry/540000?search=mitochondrial%20myopathy%EF%BC%8Cencephalopathy%EF%BC%8C%20lactic%20acidosis%EF%BC%8Cand%20stroke-like%20episodes&highlight=%22acidosi%20and%22%2C%22myopathy%20encephalopathy%22%2C%22stroke%20like%22%2Cepisode%2Clactic%2Cmitochondrial%2Cstrokelike#27); [Montagna et al., 1988](https://www.omim.org/entry/540000?search=mitochondrial%20myopathy%EF%BC%8Cencephalopathy%EF%BC%8C%20lactic%20acidosis%EF%BC%8Cand%20stroke-like%20episodes&highlight=%22acidosi%20and%22%2C%22myopathy%20encephalopathy%22%2C%22stroke%20like%22%2Cepisode%2Clactic%2Cmitochondrial%2Cstrokelike#22)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=6093682+3362373)

**Other mitochondrial encephalomyopathies include Leigh syndrome (MILS; [500017](https://www.omim.org/entry/500017)), Kearns-Sayre syndrome (KSS; [530000](https://www.omim.org/entry/530000)), MERRF syndrome ([545000](https://www.omim.org/entry/545000)), and Leber optic atrophy ([535000](https://www.omim.org/entry/535000)).**


## 致病机理
MTTL1基因

- RNALeu基因与16SrRNA基因的交界部位 （转录终止因子的结合部位）A3243G 突变转录终止因子不能结合rRNA 和mRNA合成的比例发生改变 
- 当肌肉组织中突变型mtDNA达40%-50% 时，出现眼外肌麻痹、肌病和耳聋
- 当突变型mtDNA≥90%时，可致复发性休克、痴呆、癫痫、共济失调等
## 临床表现
- 中枢神经系统的异常 
	- 阵发性头痛、复发性休克、癫痫、脑卒中样发作、痴呆、偏瘫、皮质盲等 
- 可伴有肌病、呕吐、耳聋、身材矮小等
- 常在儿童期或青年期发病

+ 安静状态下血中乳酸含量增高
+ 脑及肌肉的小动脉和毛细血管管壁有大量形态异常的线粒体（RRF）
+ 脑电图检查 – 癫痫
+ 脑部CT或MRI – 病灶不符合脑血管分布
