## 致病机理
- 肝脏线粒体过氧化物酶体生物合成因子1（ PEX1）突变，过氧化物酶体生成障碍； 
- 过氧化物酶体缺乏转运蛋白，新合成的酶分子不能进入过氧化物酶体中，超长链脂肪酸积累形成片状物，影响胚胎发育；

## 临床表现
Zellweger syndrome is an autosomal recessive systemic disorder characterized clinically by **severe neurologic dysfunction, craniofacial abnormalities, and liver dysfunction**, and biochemically **by the absence of peroxisomes**. Most severely affected individuals with classic Zellweger syndrome phenotype die within the first year of life (summary by [Wanders, 2004](https://www.omim.org/entry/214100#72)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=15098234)

- 头面部畸形，前额突出， 大囟门，枕平坦，低位耳
- 脑白质营养不良，肝硬化，多囊肾等
