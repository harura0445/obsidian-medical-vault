A number sign (#) is used with this entry because one form of Friedreich ataxia (FRDA) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous mutation in the gene encoding frataxin (FXN; [606829](https://www.omim.org/entry/606829)) on chromosome 9q21. The most common molecular abnormality is a **GAA trinucleotide repeat expansion in intron 1 of the FXN gene:** normal individuals have 5 to 30 GAA repeat expansions, whereas affected individuals have from 70 to more than 1,000 GAA triplets ([Al-Mahdawi et al., 2006](https://www.omim.org/entry/229300?search=Friedreich&highlight=friedreich#3)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=16919418)
## 致病机理
- FRDA定位于9q13 , GAA突变位于1号内含子内; Ø 正常人为在7～22
- FA病人的GAA扩展数目可达 200～ 1200。

## 临床表现
- 青春期起病、腱反射消失及深感觉缺失;
- 共济失调
