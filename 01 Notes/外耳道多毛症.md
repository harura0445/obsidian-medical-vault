## 临床表现
The hairy ears trait consists of long hairs growing from the helix of the pinna; see [Dronamraju (1964)](https://www.omim.org/entry/425500?search=Y-linked&highlight=%22y%20linked%22%2Cylinked#1) and [Stern et al. (1964)](https://www.omim.org/entry/425500?search=Y-linked&highlight=%22y%20linked%22%2Cylinked#6). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=14250426+14110028)
- 青春期外耳道部位出现变长的黑色硬毛， 长度约为 2~20 多毫米，常可伸到耳孔之外；
