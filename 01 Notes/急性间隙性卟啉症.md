A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that **acute intermittent porphyria (AIP)** is caused by heterozygous mutation in the gene encoding hydroxymethylbilane synthase (HMBS; [609806](https://www.omim.org/entry/609806)), also referred to as porphobilinogen deaminase (PBGD), on chromosome 11q23.


## 致病机理
- 胆色素原（PBG）脱氨酶突变，使细胞内δ- 氨基γ-酮戊酸（ALA)、胆色素原（PBG)不能转化为血红素； 
- 血红素含量下降反馈调节ALA合成酶表达增加，ALA和胆色素原PBG 更严重的积聚，导致疾病的发生。
## 临床表现
- 容苍白惧怕阳光牙齿尖利 “吸食鲜血 ” 神智失常
