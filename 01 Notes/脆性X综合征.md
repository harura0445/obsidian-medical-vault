A number sign (#) is used with this entry because fragile X syndrome (FXS) is caused by mutation in the FMR1 gene ([309550](https://www.omim.org/entry/309550)). The vast majority of cases are caused **by a trinucleotide (CGG)n repeat expansion** ([309550.0004](https://www.omim.org/entry/309550#0004)) of greater than 200 repeats.

See also fragile X tremor/ataxia syndrome (FXTAS; [300623](https://www.omim.org/entry/300623)), which is caused by expanded FMR1 (CGG)n repeats that range in size from 55 to 200 repeats and are referred to as 'premutations.'
## 致病机理
- Xq27.3内存在不稳定易断裂脆性部位；
- 致病基因FMR1的5´UTR内含有 CGG/精氨酸三核苷酸重复序列； 正常：6-52 异常：>200（导致FMR1基因甲基化，无法产生FMRP

## 临床表现
- 脆性X综合症患者：智能低下,皮肤松弛，关节过度伸展，长脸
