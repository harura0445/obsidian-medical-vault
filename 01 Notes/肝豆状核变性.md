A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that Wilson disease (WND) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous mutation in the ATP7B gene ([606882](https://www.omim.org/entry/606882)) on chromosome 13q14.
## 致病机理
- 致病基因：铜离子转运体 ATPase 7B（ATP7B）突变导致； 
- ATP7B位于13q14.3，主要在肝脏表达，将铜离子转运至高尔基体，将多余的铜离子排出细胞。
## 临床表现
Wilson disease (WND) is an autosomal recessive disorder characterized by **dramatic build-up of intracellular hepatic copper with subsequent hepatic and neurologic abnormalities.**

- 铜离子代谢障碍引起肝硬化、基底核损害为主的肝脑变性疾病
- **肝损伤、神经异常、角膜色素环**
