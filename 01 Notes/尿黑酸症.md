A number sign (#) is used with this entry because alkaptonuria (AKU) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous mutation in the **homogentisate 1,2-dioxygenase gene (HGD**; [607474](https://www.omim.org/entry/607474)) on chromosome 3q13.
## 致病机理
- 尿黑酸1,2双加氧酶(尿黑酸氧化酶) (homogentisate 1,2-dioxygenase gene HGD) 3q13.33突变缺失
- 由于尿黑酸氧化酶先天性缺乏，使尿黑酸不能被最终氧化成乙酰乙酸和延胡索酸，导致大量尿黑酸从尿中排出，成人期后可在体内沉着。

## 临床表现
Alkaptonuria is an **autosomal recessive metabolic disorder** characterized by **accumulation of homogentisic acid**, leading to **darkened urine, pigmentation of connective tissue (ochronosis), joint and spine arthritis, and destruction of the cardiac valves** (summary by [Vilboux et al., 2009](https://www.omim.org/entry/203500?search=alkaptonuria&highlight=alkaptonuria#41)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=19862842)

- 尿中含有尿黑酸，曝光后可变为黑色的物质
- 尿黑酸聚合物大量沉积于结缔组织（褐黄病）——皮肤，面颊、巩膜、耳廓、关节
- 关节炎、心脏病
