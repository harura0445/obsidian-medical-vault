# 甲基丙二酸尿症

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|[6p12.3](https://www.omim.org/geneMap/6/574?start=-3&limit=10&highlight=574)|Methylmalonic aciduria, mut(0) type|[251000](https://www.omim.org/entry/251000)|AR|3|MMUT|[609058](https://www.omim.org/entry/609058)|

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because methylmalonic aciduria (MMA) of the complementation group 'mut' (MAMM) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous mutation in the gene encoding methylmalonyl-CoA mutase (MUT; [609058](https://www.omim.org/entry/609058)) on chromosome 6p12.
## 致病机理
- 体内甲基丙二酰辅酶A、甲基丙二酸、丙酸等有机酸蓄积，造成神经系统损害

## 临床表现
- 严重者可引起酮症酸中毒、低血糖、高血氨、高甘氨酸血症等
