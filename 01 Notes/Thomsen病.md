A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal dominant myotonia congenita (Thomsen disease) is caused by heterozygous mutation in the gene encoding skeletal muscle chloride channel-1 (CLCN1; [118425](https://www.omim.org/entry/118425)) on chromosome 7q34.

## 临床表现
Autosomal dominant myotonia congenita is a nondystrophic skeletal muscle disorder characterized by **muscle stiffness and an inability of the muscle to relax after voluntary contraction** ([Sun et al., 2001](https://www.omim.org/entry/160800#26)). Thomsen disease is less common and less severe than Becker disease. [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=11840191)
- 肌肉强直，假性肌肉肥大
