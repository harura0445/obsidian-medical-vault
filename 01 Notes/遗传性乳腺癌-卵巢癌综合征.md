# 遗传性乳腺癌-卵巢癌综合征

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because susceptibility to familial breast-ovarian cancer-1 (BROVCA1) is caused by heterozygous germline mutations in the BRCA1 ([113705](https://www.omim.org/entry/113705)) gene on chromosomes 17q21.

## 致病机理
80%-90%的遗传性乳腺癌-卵巢癌综合征患者有 **BRCA1和BRCA2的基因突变**
