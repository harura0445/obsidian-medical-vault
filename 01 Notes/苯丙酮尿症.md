## 致病机理
- Phe无法通过PAH（苯丙氨酸羟化酶）转换成Try，而经转氨酶转换成苯丙酮酸，累积于血液中导致的高苯丙酮酸血症影响神经系统，影响患者智力发育。

## 临床表现
Phenylketonuria (PKU) is an autosomal recessive inborn error of metabolism resulting from a deficiency of **phenylalanine hydroxylase** (PAH; [612349](https://www.omim.org/entry/612349)), an enzyme that catalyzes the **hydroxylation of phenylalanine to tyrosine**, the rate-limiting step in phenylalanine catabolism. If undiagnosed and untreated, phenylketonuria can result in **impaired postnatal cognitive development** **resulting from a neurotoxic effect of hyperphenylalaninemia** ([Zurfluh et al., 2008](https://www.omim.org/entry/261600?search=Phenylketonuria&highlight=phenylketonuria#190)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=17935162)

- 精神发育迟缓 
- 皮肤、毛发和虹膜色素减退，头发呈赤褐色， 
- 癫痫，湿疹，特殊的鼠样臭味尿
