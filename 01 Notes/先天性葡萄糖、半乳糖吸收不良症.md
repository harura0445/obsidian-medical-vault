## 致病机理
小肠上皮细胞上钠依赖性葡萄糖转运体的SLC5A1基因突变→转运葡萄糖、半乳糖的膜载体蛋白SGLT1异常→葡萄糖和半乳糖吸收障碍→肠道内渗透压改变→水样腹泻

## 临床表现
Glucose/galactose malabsorption (GGM) is a rare autosomal recessive disorder caused by **a defect in glucose and galactose transport across the intestinal brush border.** Patients with GGM present with neonatal onset of severe life-threatening **watery diarrhea and dehydration**. If diagnosed and treated properly, patients can fully recover and show normal growth and development (summary by [Xin and Wang, 2011](https://www.omim.org/entry/606824?search=congenital%20glucose-galactose%20malabsorption&highlight=%22glucose%20galactose%22%2Ccongenital%2Cglucosegalactose%2Cmalabsorption#13)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=20486940)

婴儿喂食含葡萄糖和半乳糖的食物后随着腹泻加重继而出现脱水、营养不良等症状
