A number sign (#) is used with this entry because familial hypercholesterolemia-1 (FHCL1) can be caused by heterozygous, compound heterozygous, or homozygous mutation in the **low density lipoprotein receptor gene** (LDLR; [606945](https://www.omim.org/entry/606945)) on chromosome 19p13.
## 致病机理
- 低密度脂蛋白受体（Low-density lipoprotein receptor, LDLR，位于 19p13.2 ）缺陷，导致低密度脂蛋白代谢障碍，血浆总胆固醇和低密度脂蛋白-胆固醇（LDL-C）水平升高； 
- **胞对外源性胆固醇的摄取减少， 细胞内胆固醇合成增加，释放入血** 
- 过量的LDL-C沉积于吞噬细胞和其它细胞，形成黄色瘤和粥样斑块，最终导致心血管疾病的发生
## 临床表现
Familial hypercholesterolemia is characterized by **elevation of serum cholesterol bound to low density lipoprotein (LDL)**, which promotes **deposition of cholesterol in the skin (xanthelasma), tendons (xanthomas), and coronary arteries (atherosclerosis)**. The disorder occurs in 2 clinical forms: homozygous and heterozygous ([Hobbs et al., 1992](https://www.omim.org/entry/143890?search=143890&highlight=143890#69)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=1301956)

- 高胆固醇血症、特征性肌腱性黄色瘤、早发心血管疾病
