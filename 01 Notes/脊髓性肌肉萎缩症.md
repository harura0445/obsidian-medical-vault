# 脊髓性肌肉萎缩症
|Location|Phenotype|Phenotype  <br>MIM number|Inheritance|Phenotype  <br>mapping key|Gene/Locus|Gene/Locus  <br>MIM number|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|[Xq12](https://www.omim.org/geneMap/X/381?start=-3&limit=10&highlight=381)|Spinal and bulbar muscular atrophy, X-linked 1|[313200](https://www.omim.org/entry/313200)|XLR|3|AR|[313700](https://www.omim.org/entry/313700)|

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because X-linked spinal and bulbar muscular atrophy (SBMA, SMAX1), also known as Kennedy disease, is caused by **a trinucleotide CAG repeat expansion** in exon 1 of the gene encoding the androgen receptor (AR; [313700.0014](https://www.omim.org/entry/313700#0014)). CAG repeat numbers range from 38 to 62 in SBMA patients, whereas healthy individuals have 10 to 36 CAG repeats.

Mutations in the AR gene also cause androgen insensitivity syndrome (AIS; [300068](https://www.omim.org/entry/300068)).
## 致病机理
- Xq11-q12 雄激素受体蛋白AR；
- CAG40-72，正常范围 11-34
## 临床表现
- 运动神经元退化（脊髓前角细胞）造成肌肉萎缩、无力； 
- 肌萎缩为对称性、下肢较上肢严重，身体近端较远端易受侵犯。性征异常
