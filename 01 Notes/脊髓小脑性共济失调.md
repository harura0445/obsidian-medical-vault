|[6p22.3](https://www.omim.org/geneMap/6/93?start=-3&limit=10&highlight=93)|Spinocerebellar ataxia 1|[164400](https://www.omim.org/entry/164400)|AD|3|ATXN1|[601556](https://www.omim.org/entry/601556)|

A number sign (#) is used with this entry because spinocerebellar ataxia-1 (SCA1) is caused by **an expanded (CAG)n trinucleotide repeat** in the ataxin-1 gene (ATXN1; [601556](https://www.omim.org/entry/601556)) on chromosome 6p22.

## 致病机理
- 致病基因ATXN1 定位于6p22.3，其外显子中的三核昔酸(CAG)n 重复存在着动态突变所致。

## 临床表现
-  是一组由基因突变导致小脑、脑干及脊髓退行性变的神经系统遗传病
- 临床表现包括步态不稳、构音障碍及吞咽困难，晚期可出现小脑萎缩和运动能力丧失
