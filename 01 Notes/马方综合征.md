A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that Marfan syndrome (MFS) is caused by heterozygous mutation in the fibrillin-1 gene (FBN1; [134797](https://www.omim.org/entry/134797)) on chromosome 15q21.
## 致病机理
- FBN1基因突变，导致原纤维蛋白1（Fibrillin 1）的缺失
## 临床表现
A heritable disorder of fibrous connective tissue, Marfan syndrome (MFS) shows striking pleiotropism and clinical variability. The cardinal features occur in 3 systems--**skeletal, ocular, and cardiovascular** ([McKusick, 1972](https://www.omim.org/entry/154700?search=154700&highlight=154700#93); [Pyeritz and McKusick, 1979](https://www.omim.org/entry/154700?search=154700&highlight=154700#111); [Pyeritz, 1993](https://www.omim.org/entry/154700?search=154700&highlight=154700#114)).

- 遗传性结缔组织疾病，常染色体显性遗传； 
- 患病特征为四肢、手指、脚趾细长不匀称，身高明显超出常人； 
- 伴有心血管系统异常，特别是合并的心脏瓣膜异常和主动脉瘤。
