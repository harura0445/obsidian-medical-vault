A number sign (#) is used with this entry because Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease type 1B (CMT1B) is caused by heterozygous mutation in the MPZ gene ([159440](https://www.omim.org/entry/159440)) on chromosome 1q23.

Mutations in the MPZ gene can cause other sensorineural neuropathies, including Dejerine-Sottas disease ([145900](https://www.omim.org/entry/145900)), congenital hypomyelinating neuropathy ([605253](https://www.omim.org/entry/605253)), and some forms of axonal CMT type 2 (see, e.g., [607677](https://www.omim.org/entry/607677)).

## 临床表现
Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease is a sensorineural peripheral polyneuropathy. Affecting approximately 1 in 2,500 individuals, Charcot-Marie-Tooth disease is the most common inherited disorder of the **peripheral nervous system** ([Skre, 1974](https://www.omim.org/entry/118200#74)). Autosomal dominant, autosomal recessive, and X-linked forms have been recognized. [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=4430158)
