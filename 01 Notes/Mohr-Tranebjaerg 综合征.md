A number sign (#) is used with this entry because Mohr-Tranebjaerg syndrome (MTS) is caused by mutation in the DDP gene (TIMM8A; [300356](https://www.omim.org/entry/300356)) on chromosome Xq22.
## 致病机理
线粒体转运缺陷疾病，XR


## 临床表现
Mohr-Tranebjaerg syndrome (MTS) is characterized by **early childhood onset of postlingual progressive sensorineural deafness followed by progressive dystonia, mental deterioration, cortical blindness, spasticity, and psychiatric manifestations** (summary by [Ujike et al., 2001](https://www.omim.org/entry/304700?search=dystonia-deafness%20syndrome&highlight=%22dystonia%20deafness%22%2C%28syndrome%7Csyndromic%29%2Cdystoniadeafness#16)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=11405816)

- 儿童期起病的进行性感觉神经性耳聋 
- 肌张力障碍 
- 痉挛状态 
- 共济失调 
- 智力倒退 
- 进行性视力丧失等
