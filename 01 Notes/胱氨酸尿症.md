A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that cystinuria can be caused by mutation in the **SLC3A1 amino acid transporter gene** ([104614](https://www.omim.org/entry/104614)), which encodes the heavy subunit of the **renal amino acid transporter** and is located on chromosome 2p, and/or by mutation in the SLC7A9 gene ([604144](https://www.omim.org/entry/604144)), which encodes the light subunit and is located on chromosome 19. A classification scheme of cystinuria based on the molecular genetics of the disorder has been proposed (see NOMENCLATURE).
## 致病机理
膜转运蛋白缺陷(SLC3A1, SLC7A9 ) → →肾小管对氨基酸重吸收障碍 → →尿液中氨基酸含量升高 → →尿路结石

## 临床表现
Cystinuria is an autosomal disorder characterized by i**mpaired epithelial cell transport of cystine and dibasic amino acids (lysine, ornithine, and arginine) in the proximal renal tubule and gastrointestinal tract.** The impaired renal reabsorption of cystine and its low solubility **causes the formation of calculi in the urinary tract,** resulting in obstructive uropathy, pyelonephritis, and, rarely, renal failure (summary by [Barbosa et al., 2012](https://www.omim.org/entry/220100?search=Cystinuria%20type%201&highlight=1%2Ccystinuria%2Ctype#1)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=21255007)
