# 猫叫综合征
A number sign (#) is used with this entry because cri-du-chat syndrome is a well-described partial aneusomy resulting from **deletion of the short arm of chromosome 5.** There is a high probability that deletion of multiple genes is responsible for the phenotype as well as evidence that deletion of the telomerase reverse transcriptase gene (TERT; [187270](https://www.omim.org/entry/187270)) is specifically involved in the phenotypic changes of cri-du-chat syndrome.


## 致病机理
**5号染色体短臂缺失**
## 临床表现
常染色体结构异常病儿中居首位  
婴幼儿期的哭声非常似小猫的咪咪声

- 低出生体重； 
- 喉部发育异常； 
- 成年后头部较小； 
- 圆脸，下巴小，眼距宽；
- 肌肉张力低，行走和说话困难； 
- 多动，攻击性，易发脾气； 
- 严重智力障碍
