A number sign (#) is used with this entry because familial adenomatous polyposis-1 (FAP1) and its variant Gardner syndrome are caused by heterozygous mutation in the APC gene ([611731](https://www.omim.org/entry/611731)) on chromosome 5q22.

Heterozygous mutation in the APC gene promoter 1B also causes gastric adenocarcinoma and proximal polyposis of the stomach (GAPPS; [619182](https://www.omim.org/entry/619182)).

See also hereditary desmoid disease ([135290](https://www.omim.org/entry/135290)), an allelic disorder considered by some (e.g., [Lynch, 1996](https://www.omim.org/entry/175100?search=FAMILIAL%20POLYPOSIS%20OF%20THE%20COLON&highlight=%28colon%7Ccolonic%29%2Cfamilial%2Cof%2Cpolyposis%2Cthe#120)) to be a variant of FAP. [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=8940262)

## 临床表现
Familial adenomatous polyposis-1 (FAP1) is an **autosomal dominant disorder** characterized by **predisposition to cancer**. Affected individuals usually **develop hundreds to thousands of adenomatous polyps of the colon and rectum,** a small proportion of which will progress to colorectal carcinoma if not surgically treated.
