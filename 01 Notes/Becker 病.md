A number sign (#) is used with this entry because of evidence that autosomal recessive myotonia congenita (Becker disease) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous mutation in the gene encoding skeletal muscle chloride channel-1 (CLCN1; [118425](https://www.omim.org/entry/118425)) on chromosome 7q34.

Autosomal dominant myotonia congenita, or Thomsen disease ([160800](https://www.omim.org/entry/160800)), is caused by heterozygous mutation in the CLCN1 gene.

## 临床表现
Autosomal recessive myotonia congenita is a nondystrophic skeletal muscle disorder characterized by muscle stiffness and an inability of the muscle to relax after voluntary contraction. Most patients have symptom onset in the legs, which later progresses to the arms, neck, and facial muscles. **Many patients show marked hypertrophy of the lower limb muscles. Some patients show transient muscle weakness** ([Koch et al., 1993](https://www.omim.org/entry/255700#8)). Becker disease is more common and more severe than Thomsen disease. [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?cmd=link&linkname=pubmed_pubmed&from_uid=8301644)
