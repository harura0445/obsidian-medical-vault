A number sign (#) is used with this entry because cystic fibrosis (CF) is caused by homozygous or compound heterozygous **mutation in the cystic fibrosis conductance regulator gene (CFTR;** [602421](https://www.omim.org/entry/602421)) on chromosome 7q31.
## 致病机理
位于7号染色体上的囊性纤维化跨膜转运调节因子(Cystic Fibrosis Transmembrane Conductive Regulator，CFTR)基因突变**导致腺体的上皮细胞无法正常分泌氯离子**。
## 临床表现
Cystic fibrosis (CF) is classically described as a triad of **chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, exocrine pancreatic insufficiency, and elevation of sodium and chloride concentration in sweat.** Almost all males with CF are infertile due to congenital bilateral absence of the vas deferens. The disorder is associated with decreased longevity (summary by [Cutting, 2002](https://www.omim.org/entry/219700?search=cystic%20fibrosis&highlight=cystic%2Cfibrosi#56)).

全身外分泌腺细胞分泌黏液不能及时清除 → 积滞在导管和腺泡中→ 阻塞和感染→累及呼吸道、消化道、汗腺等→ 导致慢性肺部病变、胰腺外分泌功能不足、肝胆疾病和大量出汗、电解质紊乱。
