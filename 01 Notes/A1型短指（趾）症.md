## 致病机理
- 基因型：致病基因 **IHH (Indian Hedgehog)**,定位于 2q35，编码Indian hedgehog homolog，是参与成骨分化和骨生长的重要分子。
## 临床表现
Brachydactyly type A1 (BDA1) is an autosomal dominant disorder characterized by **shortening of the middle phalanges of the digits of the hand, with or without symphalangism**. Mild short stature is often present. Considerable inter- and intrafamilial variability has been observed, with all or only some digits affected, and complete absence of the middle phalanx in some cases. Metacarpals may also be shortened, and clinodactyly, camptodactyly, and ulnar deviation have been reported. Some patients exhibit abnormalities of the feet ([Zhu et al., 2007](https://www.omim.org/entry/112500?search=Brachydactyly%2CType%20A1%EF%BC%8CBDA1&highlight=%22a1%20bda1%22%2C%22brachydactyly%20type%22#22); [Lodder et al., 2008](https://www.omim.org/entry/112500?search=Brachydactyly%2CType%20A1%EF%BC%8CBDA1&highlight=%22a1%20bda1%22%2C%22brachydactyly%20type%22#13); [Byrnes et al., 2009](https://www.omim.org/entry/112500?search=Brachydactyly%2CType%20A1%EF%BC%8CBDA1&highlight=%22a1%20bda1%22%2C%22brachydactyly%20type%22#2); [Vasques et al., 2018](https://www.omim.org/entry/112500?search=Brachydactyly%2CType%20A1%EF%BC%8CBDA1&highlight=%22a1%20bda1%22%2C%22brachydactyly%20type%22#20)). [](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=18629882+19277064+29155992+17486609)

- 患者身材变矮，手变宽，所有指（趾） 骨必正常人成比例缩短； 
- 患者的中间指骨(或趾骨)短小或缺失， 与末端指(趾)融合，大拇指和大脚趾近端指(趾)骨变短； 
- 不论中间指(趾)骨缩短还是缺失，远端指(趾)关节都不能形成；
