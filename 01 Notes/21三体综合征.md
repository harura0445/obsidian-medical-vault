A number sign (#) is used with this entry because Down syndrome is caused by a chromosomal aberration, t**risomy 21**, and many genes are involved in the phenotype.
## 致病机理
- 游离型 Trisomy 21(~95%): 47, XX/XY, +21, **nondisjunction (failure separation of homologous chromosome**) during meiosis I (来自母亲：约95％；)
- 易位型 Translocation (~4%): 46, XX/XY, t(14;21), **Robertsonian translocation with part of chr.21 attached to Chr.14**
- 嵌合型 Mosaicism (~1%): 46, XX/XY;47XX/XY,+21; nondisjunction during mitosis, mixture of two types of cells with 46 or 47 chromosomes

## 临床表现
- Physical characteristics 
- Decreased muscle tone 
- Short neck, flatten facial profile and nose
- Protruding tongue
- Upward slanting eyes
- Hand features 
- Intellectual disability
- Organ defects (congenital heart disease) 

+ 智能落后、特殊面容、生长发育迟缓并伴有多发畸形
+ 易患白血病，先天性心脏病
+ 通贯掌
